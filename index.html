The mad scientist of drops.

Who is Benjamin Franklin?

(Not the guy on the 100-dollar bill. The other one.)

A Parisian DJ and producer, Benjamin Franklin has been moving house dancefloors for over 20 years. He grew up in the legendary clubs of the 90s (Les Bains Douches, La Locomotive, Le Queen) and forged a sound of his own, blending house, soul and Latin rhythms.

His “Who The Fuck Is Benjamin Franklin?” parties set dancefloors on fire from Paris to Miami via Buenos Aires. Signed to MIYU Records, he has released tracks like “This Is My House”, “Chupa Sin Mano” and “THIS SOUND”.

Then one day he thought: “The tools I need in the studio, I'll build them myself.” So he created his own plugin brand… and decided to give them away to every producer. For free. No catch. Because music is meant to be shared.

Install in 30 seconds

Promise, it's easier than tuning a kick.

  1. Download the plugin's .dmg and open it (double-click).
  2. In Finder, press ⌘ + ⇧ + G and type ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/
  3. Drag the .vst3 file into the VST3 folder, and the .component file into the Components folder.
  4. Restart your music software (Logic, Ableton, FL Studio…). Done!

The plugins are notarized by Apple: no security warning. Logic and GarageBand use the AU version (.component), everything else uses VST3.

  1. Download the plugin's .exe installer and run it.
  2. Windows may show “Windows protected your PC”: click More info → Run anyway.
  3. Click Next all the way. The plugin installs itself into C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3.
  4. Restart your music software (Ableton, FL Studio, Cubase, Reaper…). Done!

Why the warning? The installer isn't digitally signed yet (it costs a fortune). The file is safe: it comes straight from here.
