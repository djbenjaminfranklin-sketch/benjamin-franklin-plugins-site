#03

Boom!

The mad scientist of drops.

InstrumentVST3AULoad it on an instrument / MIDI track

Boom builds your drop impacts: sub boom, downlifter, uplifter. And because the sound is generated (not a stretched sample), it stays clean up to 128 bars, locked to your tempo. Riser samples never go that long and sound like a saucepan once stretched. Boom doesn't break.

  • A big BOOM! button (yes, you press it)
  • Tweakable sub boom: type, tune, length, detune
  • Rising or falling swipe, from 1 to 128 bars
  • Always in sync with your project's tempo

Boom is an instrument, not an effect. You won't find it among the insert effects: create an instrument track and pick Benjamin Franklin › Boom. In Cubase: Project › Add Track › Instrument. In Ableton: Instruments. In Logic: Software Instrument › AU Instruments.

Install in 30 seconds

Promise, it's easier than tuning a kick.

  1. Download the plugin's .dmg and open it (double-click).
  2. In Finder, press ⌘ + ⇧ + G and type ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/
  3. Drag the .vst3 file into the VST3 folder, and the .component file into the Components folder.
  4. Restart your music software (Logic, Ableton, FL Studio…). Done!

The plugins are notarized by Apple: no security warning. Logic and GarageBand use the AU version (.component), everything else uses VST3.

  1. Download the plugin's .exe installer and run it.
  2. Windows may show “Windows protected your PC”: click More info → Run anyway.
  3. Click Next all the way. The plugin installs itself into C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3.
  4. Restart your music software (Ableton, FL Studio, Cubase, Reaper…). Done!

Why the warning? The installer isn't digitally signed yet (it costs a fortune). The file is safe: it comes straight from here.

FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. No subscription, no account, no email to hand over, no hidden “pro” version. Download, produce.

Can I use them in commercial tracks?

Yes. Use them on anything you release, sell or stream — no royalties, no credit required, no restrictions. (A shout-out is always appreciated!)

Which DAWs does it work in?

Logic Pro and GarageBand (Mac, AU), plus Ableton Live, Cubase, Nuendo, FL Studio, Studio One, Bitwig and Reaper (Mac and Windows, VST3; Ableton also in AU on Mac). Plug de fou, SlowMo and PUMP are effects (insert them on an audio track); Boom is an instrument (load it on an instrument / MIDI track). Pro Tools? Not yet — it needs the AAX format.

Does it run on my M4 / M5 Mac?

Yes: Apple Silicon (M1 to M5) and Intel Macs, macOS 12 and up.

What about Linux? iPad?

Not for now. Mac and Windows only.
